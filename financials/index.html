Direct state exposure
$56M

A $38M appropriation plus $18M in bonds that the resort repays from its own revenue. The state guarantee is a backstop only.

Why the road is separate
$22M

The 3 to 4 mile access road is a year-round public corridor. It belongs in a transportation funding conversation, not on the resort's balance sheet.

Contingency is built in
$18M

The $17.9M construction contingency, about 22% of hard costs, is already inside the $104.19M estimate. It is a cost line, not an extra ask.

The ask in context

A one-time investment.

Not a tax increase. The $56M direct state share is a one-time investment from existing revenue. In the 2024-25 season alone, skiing generated $342.6M in state and local tax. This is a small slice of that, paid once.

Not a first for Utah

The state already builds places to play.

In just two sessions, the Legislature put more than $185M into parks and recreation, more than this project's full $104.19M build. The direct state share here, $56M, is less than the $60M approved for the Bear Lake marina alone.
